# Go-to-market, full read (2026-11-09). Adds to part 8 of 2026-11-08-giants-governor.md; the daily round turns each into a queue item, then deletes this file.
Ground rules for every item: the commercial lead runs selling and outreach under the brand (a pen name is fine), never the owner; nothing from the owner's employment (clients, audits, internal frameworks, anonymised stories from work) is used; every number shown to a buyer (customers, ratings, counts, testimonials) comes from real data with consent; no fake timers, stock counts, ratings or reviews.
- gtm-seo-technical (2026-12-05): static site under two seconds on a phone; sitemap submitted to Google Search Console; privacy-first analytics (self-hosted or Plausible-style, no cookies, DPDP-safe; not Google Analytics); Product, Course, FAQ and Article schema; every article links two or three related articles and one product; HTTPS; a test for title (60) and description (155) lengths, one H1, alt text, schema and a current sitemap.
- gtm-seo-keywords (2026-12-05): a keyword map in three tiers (buyer intent -> product page; research intent -> guide with email capture; awareness -> video and posts), validated with a search-volume tool by the commercial lead; a 12-week content calendar from it.
- gtm-backlinks (2027-01-15): guest articles on respected security publications and associations, by the commercial lead.
- gtm-youtube-seo (2026-12-31): video template: keyword title, first two description lines with the free-resource link, chapters, pinned comment, cards, end screen; the first 20 videos answer real searches.
- gtm-linkedin-cadence (2026-12-15): three posts a week (Monday teach, Wednesday story, Friday call to action), carousels and polls; 15 minutes a day of useful comments on buyers' posts; drafts by the local or open models, posted by the commercial lead.
- gtm-content-repurpose (2026-12-15): one idea -> seven pieces (article, video script, carousel, thread, newsletter, long article, 60-second audiogram), generated by the estate's own models from one outline, each linking to the product; a person approves before publishing.
- gtm-lead-magnets (2026-12-10): four free downloads (compliance checklist, exam questions, career guide, risk dashboard spreadsheet) plus the free tool tier; one simple landing page each (headline, email field, download).
- gtm-email-sequence (2026-12-15; details for gtm-email-funnel): day 0 deliver, 2 value, 5 proof, 7 soft offer, 10 FAQ, 14 offer with a real launch price, 21 last call, then a weekly newsletter with one offer in four emails. Prefer a self-hosted open-source list server (giants principle, privacy); double opt-in, one-tap unsubscribe; targets open 30%, click 5%, list-to-purchase 3-5%.
- gtm-pricing-psychology (2026-11-30; with gtm-offer-ladder): three plans (basic, popular middle, bundle); anchor against the real cost of a consultant or of building it yourself; charm prices; annual plan with a saving; team seats; an invoice request for banks and government; instalments for courses; version-locked launch price. Urgency only when real (a cohort's seats, a launch window, a regulator's audit season).
- gtm-checkout-killers (2026-12-15; details for gtm-checkout-friction): guest checkout, one page, local currency, instalments, instant delivery, perfect on a phone, 30-day refund, abandoned-cart recovery, sign in with an existing account (one door); payment order by market (UPI first in India, cards and PayPal elsewhere). Business income to a separate account from the salary; a chartered accountant confirms the setup.
- gtm-post-purchase (2026-12-31): instant delivery, a 5-minute quick start, day 3 tips and an unadvertised bonus, day 7 two-question survey (score of 8 or more: ask for a testimonial with consent; low score: personal help), day 14 case study and related product, day 30 referral reminder.
- gtm-referrals (2027-01-15; with gtm-affiliates-community): customer referral links where both sides win; affiliates (bloggers, trainers, newsletters, educators) at a published commission. Reviews: never pay or discount for a review where the platform forbids it, and follow India's rules on fake and incentivised reviews (disclosed only).
- gtm-community (2027-01-15): a free community run on the estate's own Mattermost first (no new dependency), channels for circulars (regwatch posts summaries), help, careers and announcements; moderated; its questions feed the product queue.
- gtm-partnerships (2027-01-31): audit firms (white-label), banking institutes (course licensing), security vendors (sponsorship), associations (bulk training); each checked for conflicts with the owner's employer first; run by the commercial lead.
- gtm-plg-watermark (2026-12-31): the free tier of the readiness and circular tools is generous, puts a small 'made with SecuRAX' footer on what it exports, and shows a one-click upgrade at the real limit (count, export format or team access).
- gtm-dashboard (2026-12-15; details for gtm-metrics): traffic (site, video, followers, list size), conversion (opt-in 5-10%, list-to-purchase 3-5%, checkout completion 70%+, cart recovery, refunds under 5%) and revenue (4-week rolling, per product, per channel, average order, lifetime value, acquisition cost), weekly in ./securax venture.
- gtm-30-day-launch (2026-11-30): the commercial lead's first 30 days, about 1.2 hours a day: brand accounts, site with home, products and blog, first template pack, first guide and lead magnet, first video, email capture and sequence, second pack, five posts, affiliate programme, review on day 30. Only after gtm-claims-check and the trademark filing.
- gtm-honesty-guard (2026-12-15): a test on the site and product pages that fails any customer count, rating, 'trusted by' or countdown not backed by a data file, so a fake number can never ship.
