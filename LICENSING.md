**From v0.4.0 on, SecuRAX is licensed under the [Functional Source License, Version 1.1, MIT Future License](LICENSE) (FSL-1.1-MIT).**
Versions up to and including v0.3.0 were released under the MIT licence, and they stay under it.

## What you may do

- Use it for yourself, your family or your organisation, for any internal purpose, at no cost.
- Read, study and change the code, and run your changed copy.
- Use it for non-commercial teaching and research.
- Provide professional services to someone who runs SecuRAX under these terms, for example installing, configuring, auditing
  or supporting it.

## What you may not do

Sell it, or a product or service built from it, that competes with SecuRAX: one that replaces it, replaces a product we offer
with it, or offers the same or substantially similar functionality (the licence calls this a Competing Use).

## Each version becomes MIT after two years

Two years after a version is released, that version is also available under the MIT licence. A two-year-old release is fully
open source. The newest work funds the people who build it.

## Why

- **Free where it matters:** everything a person or a team needs to be safe stays free to use, and the code stays readable and
  auditable. Regulated buyers expect both.
- **Not free to resell:** a commercial copy of the newest version would take the work without funding it.
