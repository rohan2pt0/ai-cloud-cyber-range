You decide where it runs

Nothing is opened to the internet. Devices reach the estate over your own WireGuard tailnet, and the data stays on machines you own.

  • Laptop: Windows (WSL), macOS or Linux, 16 GB RAM, sized automatically.
  • Home server: lend a stronger machine as the "home" model rung. It counts as local for residency.
  • Offline / air-gapped: threat intelligence snapshots, a local model floor, an app that works offline.
  • Your cloud, in India: Terraform for AWS, GCP and Azure India regions, dormant until you choose to grow.

Questions

Does my data leave my machine?

Not unless you allow it. With residency enabled, personal and bank-related data is answered only by the local model. Cloud models are optional and get only what policy allows.

What does it cost to run?

Nothing. The default ladders use free tiers and end on a local model. A paid model is opt-in and capped by a monthly budget.

Is it certified?

No. It is mapped to RBI, SEBI CSCRF, NIST AI 600-1 and CCSP with linked evidence, but it has not been independently audited. Treat it as a well-instrumented lab and personal estate, not a certified product.

Can I use it on a bank-issued laptop?

No. Install it only on hardware you personally own, and never connect it to employer systems.

How do I contribute?

Open an issue or a pull request on GitHub. Every change must pass the test suite, the real-estate sandbox and the build contract.
