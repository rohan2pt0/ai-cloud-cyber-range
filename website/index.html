Questions security teams ask

How is it deployed?

On your own hardware, as containers, in an afternoon. Your applications point to one OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Nothing is published to the internet.

Does it replace our DLP?

No. It adds the AI-specific layer DLP does not see: prompt attacks, model routing and an audit trail of every AI request and answer.

Which models can our people use?

Any provider with an OpenAI-compatible API, plus local models on your own servers. You decide which data classes each model may see.

Is it certified?

Not yet. It is mapped to RBI, SEBI CSCRF, NIST AI 600-1 and CCSP with linked evidence, but it has not been independently audited. Design partners can review every test themselves.

What will it cost after the design-partner period?

A per-user subscription with a founding discount for design partners. The open-source edition stays free.
