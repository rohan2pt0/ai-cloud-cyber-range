## 1. Purpose
To set how {{ORG}} prevents, detects, responds to and recovers from cyber attacks, so that critical services keep running
and incidents are reported on time. It sits under the Information Security Policy.

## 2. Commitments
1. **Know what we have:** a complete inventory of internet-facing and internal assets, reconciled monthly.
2. **Reduce exposure:** a minimal attack surface, segmented networks, hardened systems, timely patching.
3. **Protect identity:** MFA, privileged access management, and a protected directory.
4. **Detect:** central logging, 24x7 monitoring by an in-house or managed SOC, use cases mapped to MITRE ATT&CK.
5. **Respond:** an incident response plan and a cyber crisis management plan, exercised at least twice a year.
6. **Recover:** recovery time and recovery point objectives for every critical service, met in tested drills.
7. **Report:** regulators and authorities are informed within their deadlines (CERT-In: 6 hours from detection).
8. **Learn:** every significant incident and exercise ends with a lessons-learned review and tracked actions.

## 3. Cyber resilience metrics (reported quarterly)
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Critical and high vulnerabilities closed within SLA | 95% or more |
| Endpoints with active EDR | 99% or more |
| Privileged accounts under PAM | 100% |
| Mean time to detect / to respond (critical incidents) | set per year, trending down |
| Critical services with a passed DR test in the last 12 months | 100% |
| Staff who completed awareness training / phishing click rate | 100% / under 5% |

## 4. Review
Reviewed yearly, and after any major incident or regulatory change.
