# {{ORG}} · Information Security Policy

| Owner | Approved by | Version | Effective | Next review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) | Board / Board committee | 1.0 | {{DATE}} | one year after approval |

## 1. Purpose
To protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of {{ORG}}'s information, systems and services, to meet legal,
regulatory and contractual duties, and to keep the trust of customers, staff and partners.

## 2. Scope
All information {{ORG}} owns or holds, in any form; every system, network, device, application and cloud service used for it;
every employee, contractor, consultant and third party who can reach them.

## 3. Principles
1. **Risk-based:** controls follow from the risk to the business, judged by criticality and data sensitivity.
2. **Least privilege and need to know:** access is the minimum needed, approved, reviewed and removed on time.
3. **Defence in depth:** no single control is relied on alone.
4. **Secure by design:** security is part of every project and change from the start.
5. **Accountability:** every asset, risk and exception has a named owner.
6. **Assume breach:** detect fast, respond to a rehearsed plan, recover from tested backups.

## 4. Roles
| Role | Accountable for |
|---|---|
| Board / Board committee | Approving this policy and the risk appetite; receiving quarterly reports |
| CEO / senior management | Resourcing the programme; making business owners accountable |
| CISO | The security programme, this policy, reporting, and independent oversight of controls |
| Steering committee | Quarterly review of risks, exceptions, incidents and progress |
| Business and asset owners | Classifying their assets, accepting residual risk, closing findings in time |
| IT and engineering | Building and running systems to the security standards |
| Every user | Following this policy and the acceptable use policy; reporting incidents at once |

## 5. Policy statements
| Area | Statement |
|---|---|
| Asset management | Every asset is inventoried with an owner, a criticality rating and a data classification |
| Data classification | Data is classified Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted and handled accordingly |
| Access control | Access is role-based, approved, reviewed quarterly for critical systems and removed the same day at exit; MFA protects remote, email, admin and critical access |
| Cryptography | Restricted and Confidential data is encrypted at rest and in transit with approved algorithms; keys are managed apart from data |
| Operations security | Systems are hardened, patched to SLA, protected against malware, and logged centrally for at least 180 days |
| Vulnerability management | Weaknesses are found, rated and fixed within the SLAs of the vulnerability management SOP |
| Change management | Changes are risk-assessed, approved, tested and reversible |
| Exceptions | Any deviation needs a documented risk assessment, an owner, an end date and a compensating control |
| Secure development | Security requirements, threat modelling and testing apply to all software and APIs before release |
| Suppliers | Third parties are assessed before onboarding and reviewed by risk tier; contracts carry security clauses |
| Incident management | Incidents are reported at once, handled to the incident response SOP, and reported to regulators within their deadlines |
| Business continuity | Critical services have tested continuity and recovery plans; backups follow 3-2-1-1-0 and are restore-tested |
| Awareness | Everyone completes security awareness training on joining and every year |
| Compliance | {{ORG}} meets applicable laws and regulations (for example the IT Act, CERT-In directions, the DPDP Act and sector regulators' rules) |

## 6. Compliance and exceptions
Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action. Exceptions follow the change and exception SOP.

## 7. Review
Reviewed at least yearly, and after any major incident, regulatory change or business change.
