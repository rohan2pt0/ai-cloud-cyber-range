## 2. Phases
1. **Detect and report:** anyone reports to the SOC; the SOC triages within 15 minutes.
2. **Contain:** isolate affected systems and accounts; preserve evidence first (memory, logs, disk images).
3. **Notify:** regulators and authorities within their deadlines (CERT-In: 6 hours from detection; sector regulators as they require); customers when the law requires.
4. **Eradicate:** remove the cause; reset credentials; close the entry point.
5. **Recover:** restore from clean, verified backups; monitor closely.
6. **Learn:** blameless post-incident review within 10 working days; actions with owners and dates.
