## 1. Change
| Type | Approval | Security review |
|---|---|---|
| Standard (pre-approved, low risk) | Change manager | Pre-approved template |
| Normal | Change advisory board | Required for P1/P2 assets, firewall, identity and internet-facing changes |
| Emergency | Two approvers, documented within 24 hours | After the fact, within 3 days |

Every change has a test result and a back-out plan.

## 2. Security exceptions
An exception is any deviation from a policy or standard.

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| What deviates, and why | yes |
| Risk assessment (likelihood, impact, asset priority) | yes |
| Compensating control | yes |
| Business owner who accepts the risk | yes |
| End date (no more than 12 months) | yes |
| Approval by the CISO (and the steering committee for high risks) | yes |

Exceptions are tracked in the exception register, reported monthly, and expire on their end date unless renewed with a new assessment.
