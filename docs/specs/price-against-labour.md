# Spec: price-against-labour

Queue item `price-against-labour` in `governance/queue.json` · due 2026-12-09 · added 2026-11-11.

**The ask, as the queue holds it:** Owner, 2026-11-11 (a cue from a post read on the phone, in our words): a consumer product is priced against its rivals, a capital good against the labour and waiting it removes. What a premium tool sells is deletion: of waiting, of a fixed salary, of human error. Apply it to SecuRAX

## Outcome for the person

A buyer understands the price as the hours, fees and mistakes removed, and a user sees what the app took off their plate this month.

## Data

Counts the estate already has: reminders sent, renewals caught, checks run. Pricing notes stay private.

## Screen (one action)

In the monthly progress message: one line, `Taken off your plate this month`, with one button to see the list.

## Test that proves it

A unit test that each figure comes from a real counter and is never estimated upward; no figure shows when the counter is empty.

## Evolution rail (amoeba to enterprise)

- **Amoeba, today:** A monthly sum on the device.
- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** Per-team value reports that feed renewal conversations, by consent.
