Any Indian firm can see which DPDP obligations it meets and what to do next, well before they apply.

A requirement-level map (cited by reference, paraphrased) on the comply and readiness engine.

## Test that proves it

- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** Sector overlays, a signed official version and a consultant import.
