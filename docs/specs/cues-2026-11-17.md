## Already in SecuRAX (nothing to build)

- **A private mesh to every machine and the phone (Tailscale, no open ports):** the estate already uses it, with names, not ports.
- **Own your AI (local models, the cloud optional):** the gateway's ladder ends on a local model; sensitive data stays local.
- **Trivy, Semgrep, gitleaks and garak** run in CI; the purple-team loop covers PyRIT's ground.
- **Memory that turns repeated corrections into rules:** `LESSONS.md`, written by autobuild when a red attempt turns green.
- **An owned channel beats an ad budget (the founder-brand post):** the personal-brand system in queue `life-systems`.

## Skipped, and why

- **Agent-swarm and "second brain" repo lists:** unverified claims, untrusted code, and we already run planner, builder and reviewer with a router.
- **Reverse-engineering tools (rea, mitmproxy, Ghidra) and a bug-bounty report archive:** useful to a researcher, not a vital few for the person; a range scenario may borrow them later.
- **A faster inference engine with shared KV cache:** a data-centre concern; the estate runs on one person's machines.
