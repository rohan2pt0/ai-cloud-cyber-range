# Spec: design-premium-onboarding

Queue item `design-premium-onboarding` in `governance/queue.json` · due 2026-12-20 · added 2026-11-09.

**The ask, as the queue holds it:** A white-glove first run: a short welcome, name and goals in three taps, guest first, a 'your estate is ready' moment, privacy shown as a luxury

## Outcome for the person

A newcomer feels looked after in the first thirty seconds and reaches a first win.

## Data

Name and goals (three taps), kept on the device; guest first.

## Screen (one action)

Three screens, each with one tap; the last says `Your estate is ready`.

## Test that proves it

A device audit run of the first run: under three taps to value, no typing required, both themes.

## Evolution rail (amoeba to enterprise)

- **Amoeba, today:** A three-tap welcome.
- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** A guided set-up for a household or office with roles.
