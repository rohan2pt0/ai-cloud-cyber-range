# Spec: para-systems

Queue item `para-systems` in `governance/queue.json` · due 2026-12-09 · added 2026-11-11.

**The ask, as the queue holds it:** Owner, 2026-11-11: use PARA and the best methods of great firms, consultancies and the lessons of failed products across SecuRAX and its apps, so the systems deliver anything desired, 80/20 first

## Outcome for the person

The person finds anything in two taps, because everything lives under Projects, Areas, Resources or Archive.

## Data

A shelf field on each item (project, area, resource, archive) with a default the app chooses; doc 135 holds the decision.

## Screen (one action)

In the Inbox: each item shows its suggested shelf with one button, `File it`.

## Test that proves it

A jsdom test per slice: the More menu groups under four headings; one tap files an item; archived items leave every active list.

## Evolution rail (amoeba to enterprise)

- **Amoeba, today:** Grouping and a default shelf on the device.
- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** Shared shelves for a family or team, with retention rules on the Archive.
