# Spec: i18n-quality

Queue item `i18n-quality` in `governance/queue.json` · due 2027-01-31 · added 2026-11-13.

**The ask, as the queue holds it:** Gujarati (and every Indian language) good enough to win business communities and UHNW families who read little: accurate, complete, spoken aloud, and the same quality for national and global audiences

## Outcome for the person

A person who reads little can use the app in their own language, or hear it.

## Data

Whole-sentence templates with placeholders, a glossary, coverage per screen.

## Screen (one action)

The Listen button; the language switch.

## Test that proves it

`test89.js` as the quality gate: coverage of visible text, placeholders intact, no fragment joined in code.

## Evolution rail (amoeba to enterprise)

- **Amoeba, today:** Catalogs for the 80/20.
- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** Reviewed catalogs and voice for every launch language.
