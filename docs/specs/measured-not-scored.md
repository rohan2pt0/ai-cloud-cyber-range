# Spec: measured-not-scored

Queue item `measured-not-scored` in `governance/queue.json` · due 2026-11-28 · added 2026-11-14.

**The ask, as the queue holds it:** Make the Life OS numbers come from real sources, not self-scoring, with the least friction (a friend's feedback)

## Outcome for the person

The numbers come from real statements and devices, so the person trusts them without rating themselves.

## Data

Messages and statements shared in and read on the device; ticks already recorded.

## Screen (one action)

After a share: the reading, one button, `Accept`.

## Test that proves it

Tests with synthetic statements; nothing leaves the device; card due dates reach the calendar.

## Evolution rail (amoeba to enterprise)

- **Amoeba, today:** Paste or share.
- **Enterprise, when conditions call for it:** Connected sources by consent with the narrowest permission.
