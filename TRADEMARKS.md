# Names and logos

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the code. It does not cover the product name, the brand name or any logo (the names are
set in `brand.env`).

You may:
- use, change and sell the code, as the licence says;
- say truthfully that your work is based on this project, or works with it.

Please do not:
- ship a changed copy under this project's names or logos (use your own name for a fork);
- suggest that the project, or any regulator, approves or certifies your product or service.

The names of regulators, standards bodies and other projects appear only to say what a map or a connector covers. None of
them endorses this project.

Questions: the contact address in `brand.env`.
